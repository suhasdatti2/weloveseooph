Title: The Nintendo Entertainment System: Revolution, Reaction, and Reform in the Video Game Industry
Division: [Junior / Senior] Division
Category: Group (or Individual) Website

1. How did you choose your topic?

We chose the Nintendo Entertainment System because we are both interested in video games and wanted to know how the industry we enjoy today actually began. When we learned that video games had nearly disappeared in 1983 and that one console brought them back, we realized the NES was a perfect fit for the theme "Revolution, Reaction, Reform in History." It let us study a turning point that we genuinely cared about.

2. How did you conduct your research?

We began with secondary sources to build background knowledge, including books and articles on the history of video games and the 1983 crash. We then looked for primary sources from the launch period, such as original NES advertisements, packaging, instruction manuals, and archival materials preserved by the Video Game History Foundation. We compared statistics across multiple sources to make sure the figures we used, like the industry's revenue collapse, were accurate.

3. How did you create your project?

We chose the website category because it let us combine text, images, and timelines to tell the story clearly. We organized our site directly around the theme, with separate sections for the crash, the revolution, the reforms, and the lasting legacy. We wrote and revised our analysis together, kept our word count within the limit, and credited every source in our annotated bibliography.

4. How does your project relate to the theme?

Our topic embodies all three parts of the theme. The NES was a reaction to the 1983 video game crash; it succeeded through reform, including third-party licensing, the 10NES lockout chip, and the Seal of Quality; and it created a lasting revolution by reviving the industry, establishing the modern console business model, and making video games a permanent part of global culture.
